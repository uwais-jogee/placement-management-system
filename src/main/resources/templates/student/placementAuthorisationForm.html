New Placement Authorisation Request

By submitting this form, you confirm that you will meet the following requirements for your placement:

  • Act professionally and responsibly, both in your studies and on placement.
  • Inform the University if you have any queries or concerns about your placement or Placement Provider.
  • Update the University immediately if your placement start or end date changes.

After submitting this form, an email will be sent to your contact at the placement providing company requesting additional information.

Once your placement is confirmed, you will be assigned a tutor who will serve as your primary contact throughout the placement. Your tutor may also schedule a visit during your placement.
